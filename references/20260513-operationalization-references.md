# Operational Hypothesis

The enterprise AI ecosystem is rapidly evolving specialized operational layers:
- AIRE
- LLMOps
- AgentOps
- observability
- runtime governance
- evaluations
- orchestration

However, enterprises still appear to struggle operationalizing these layers coherently across:
- runtime safety
- governance
- recoverability
- deployment readiness
- human oversight
- operational control

AISafeDeploy is an attempt to study and organize these operational concerns into a more coherent operational framework for autonomous enterprise AI systems.
